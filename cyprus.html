Why Study in Cyprus

A European education, close to home

Cyprus has become an increasingly popular study destination for international students seeking a European academic environment. Programs are widely taught in English, institutions are internationally oriented, and the island offers a comparatively affordable cost of living alongside a warm climate and strong sense of community.

  • English-taught programs across most institutions
  • A European academic environment and qualification
  • Comparatively affordable tuition and living costs
  • A large, welcoming international student community
